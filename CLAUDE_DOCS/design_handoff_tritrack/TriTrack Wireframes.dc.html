1 · Time window drawn as a numeric stepper, not a scroll wheel. Doc mandates wheels for "categorical" selections; a duration reads more as numeric/continuous. Confirm stepper (or a slider) is fine here, or you want a wheel for consistency.
2 · Extent is now an editable polygon, not just a rectangle: drag any corner or the small hollow midpoint dots to bow an edge outward.
